Case studies Manufacturing / RMG

From register books to live production data across six garment floors

An ERP and manufacturing execution system covering order costing, production tracking, quality and compliance for a 6,200-worker garments group.

ClientTextura Mills
SectorManufacturing / RMG

The challenge

Textura costed orders in spreadsheets built by one person who had worked there for nineteen years. Production status came from hourly register entries walked to the office, which meant the floor was always reporting the past.

Buyer compliance audits required evidence — attendance, overtime, wage records — scattered across paper registers and three disconnected systems. Preparing for one audit took a team of four most of a month.

How we approached it

01

Cost the order correctly first

We rebuilt order costing as the foundation, because every downstream decision — line allocation, overtime, margin — depends on knowing the true cost per garment.

02

Data capture at the line

Tablet terminals at each line with a three-tap interface, designed with supervisors so it took less time than the register book it replaced.

03

Live efficiency dashboards

Hourly target versus actual per line, visible on floor displays and on the management dashboard at the same moment.

04

Compliance as a by-product

Attendance, overtime and wage data captured once, generating buyer audit evidence automatically rather than as a separate exercise.

What we built

  • Order costing engine with material, CM and overhead breakdown
  • Line-level production capture on tablets with a three-tap interface
  • Live efficiency, WIP and bottleneck dashboards on floor displays
  • Quality defect capture with Pareto analysis by operation
  • Attendance, overtime and payroll integrated to compliance reporting
  • Buyer audit pack generated on demand from live records
