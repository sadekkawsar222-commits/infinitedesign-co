About Infinite Designs

Four people, one year, forty projects — and no layer between you and them.

Infinite Designs is a digital product studio in Dhaka. We are new, we are deliberately small, and both of those are the point: the person who scopes your project is the person who designs it, builds it and answers the phone afterwards.

Our story

Infinite Designs began in 2024, when four of us decided we would rather build software the way we thought it should be built than keep explaining why it was not being built that way. Between us we had spent years inside larger companies watching good projects slow down in handovers — a designer who never met the engineer, an account manager relaying decisions, a client who could not get a straight answer about a deadline.

So we started a studio with none of that. Four people, two projects at a time, and a rule that the client talks directly to whoever is doing the work. In our first year that has produced more than forty delivered projects for eighteen clients in Bangladesh, the UAE, the UK and Australia.

What we do has not changed since the first contract: custom software, web platforms, mobile applications, AI integration, ERP and HRM systems, e-commerce and LMS builds. What we are careful about is how much of it we take on at once. A small studio that finishes things is more useful to you than a large one that starts them.

We are honest about being young. We do not have a decade of case studies, and where a project needs specialist depth we do not have, we say so and help you find it rather than learning on your budget.

Our mission

Make good software reachable for companies our size.

Serious digital work in Bangladesh has mostly been priced and structured for large organisations. A growing company with a real problem — a distributor drowning in spreadsheets, a clinic with no patient record, a retailer whose stock figures disagree — is offered either an enterprise programme it cannot afford or a freelancer who disappears after launch.

Our mission is to sit in between: proper discovery, proper architecture, proper handover, at a scale and price a growing company can actually commit to. We want every client to finish an engagement owning something they understand and a team that can keep changing it.

Our vision

Be the studio clients recommend without being asked.

We are one year old, so our ambition is not to be the biggest studio in Dhaka. It is to reach the point where most of our work arrives because someone we built for told someone else to call us. That is a slower way to grow and a much harder one to fake.

Getting there means investing in the parts clients rarely see — test coverage, documentation, threat models, evaluation sets — because those are what separate a system that keeps working from one that quietly rots after handover.

  • The people who pitch are the people who build
  • Written scope, written architecture decisions, written trade-offs
  • A handover that leaves your team able to continue without us
What we hold to

Six things we will not trade away.

These are the commitments clients can hold us to, and the ones we recruit against.

Say the difficult thing early

If a deadline is at risk or a requested feature is a mistake, you hear it in the weekly call, not in the retrospective. Uncomfortable clarity is cheaper than comfortable silence.

Measure what we claim

Every result on this site is a number someone can check. We agree the metric before the work starts and report it honestly afterwards, including when it disappoints.

Design for the median device

Not the flagship, not the fibre line. If it does not work on a four-year-old Android handset in the evening peak, it does not work.

Leave a team stronger than we found it

Code in your repositories, documentation you can read, and engineers who can change the system without us. Dependency is not a business model.

Security is a design decision

Threat modelling happens in discovery, not in a review two weeks before launch. Authorisation logic is designed, not inherited from a framework default.

Finish things

A studio full of 90%-complete projects helps nobody. We limit concurrent work so that what we start reaches production and gets supported.

Milestones

The first eighteen months, in order.

A short history, honestly told — each of these changed how the studio works.

Mar 2025

The decision

Four of us — two engineers, two designers — agreed to leave and start something where the person who scopes the work also builds it.

Jul 2025

Studio opens in Gulshan

Infinite Designs registered, one room, four desks. The first contract was a retail POS rebuild that arrived through a former colleague.

Oct 2025

First ten projects delivered

Mostly web platforms and mobile apps for Dhaka businesses. We set the two-projects-at-a-time rule after briefly breaking it.

Jan 2026

First client abroad

A SaaS engagement for a client in the UAE, followed by work in the UK and Australia. We wrote the four-hour overlap guarantee into the contract template.

Apr 2026

AI and data work begins

Our first retrieval and forecasting engagements, built evaluation-first from the start because we had watched the alternative fail elsewhere.

Sep 2026

Forty projects in, still four people

Eighteen clients across four markets. Hiring our next three deliberately slowly — the studio stays small on purpose.
