A payment orchestration layer that survives when a provider does not

A multi-tenant orchestration platform routing card, wallet and mobile financial service payments across eleven providers with automatic failover.

The challenge

Lumenpay’s merchants integrated one gateway each. When that gateway degraded — which happened on most large sale days — checkout simply failed, and the merchant found out from angry customers rather than from a dashboard.

Reconciliation was manual. Finance teams at merchant companies spent two to three days a month matching settlement files against orders, and disputes took weeks to resolve because nobody held a single ledger.

How we approached it

01

Provider abstraction first

A single API surface over eleven providers, with each provider’s quirks isolated behind an adapter and covered by contract tests.

02

Health-based routing

Continuous success-rate monitoring per provider and per instrument, with automatic rerouting when a provider degrades below threshold.

03

A ledger merchants can trust

Double-entry ledger with settlement matching, so reconciliation is a report rather than a spreadsheet exercise.

04

Self-serve onboarding

Merchants integrate, test in sandbox and go live without a sales call, which cut Lumenpay’s cost of acquisition in half.

What we built

  • One API across eleven payment providers with adapter-level isolation
  • Health-based routing with automatic failover in under 400 ms
  • Double-entry ledger with automated settlement reconciliation
  • Merchant self-serve onboarding, sandbox and go-live
  • PCI-DSS aligned architecture with tokenised card storage
  • Per-merchant analytics on authorisation rates by instrument
