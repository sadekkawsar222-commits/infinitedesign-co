How engagements run

Five stages, whichever services you need.

01

Discover

1–3 weeks · Research report, prioritised findings

Interviews, observation and data review with the people who will use the thing. We look for the constraint that actually governs the outcome, which is rarely the one in the brief.

02

Define

1–2 weeks · Scope document, architecture decisions

Scope, architecture and success metrics agreed in writing. This is where we say no to features, and where the metric we will be judged on gets fixed.

03

Design

3–6 weeks · Prototype, design system, test findings

Prototypes tested with real users before a line of production code exists. Design continues alongside build — it does not end at handover.

04

Build

6–24 weeks · Working software every fortnight

Two-week sprints with a working demo at the end of each. You see progress in the product, not in a status report.

05

Launch & evolve

Ongoing · Release, monitoring, 90-day support

Staged rollout, monitoring and a measured comparison against the metric agreed in Define. Then a backlog shaped by what the data shows.
