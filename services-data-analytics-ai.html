Talk about Data Analytics & AI-Driven Solutions

Before analytics comes agreement. Most organisations we meet have four systems producing four different revenue numbers. We build the pipelines and the definitions that end that argument, then put the result in front of decision-makers daily.

Once the foundation holds, prediction becomes worth doing — demand forecasts, churn scores, credit risk, inventory optimisation — each wired into an operational action rather than a slide.

What the work includes

  • Data warehouse and lakehouse architecture
  • ETL/ELT pipelines with tested transformations
  • Metric definitions and a governed semantic layer
  • Executive, operational and self-serve dashboards
  • Forecasting, segmentation and propensity models
  • Real-time streaming and alerting
  • Data quality monitoring and lineage
  • Analyst enablement and SQL training

What you receive

  • Warehouse with documented, tested models
  • Dashboard suite for each decision-making group
  • Model services with monitoring and retraining plan
  • Data dictionary and governance handbook

Questions we get asked
