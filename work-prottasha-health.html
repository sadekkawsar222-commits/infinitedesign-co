One patient record across nine hospitals and a telemedicine network

A unified health information system and teleconsultation platform connecting nine facilities, 640 clinicians and 1.1 million patient records.

Location

Dhaka & Chattogram

The challenge

Prottasha operated nine facilities on four different systems. A patient treated in Chattogram arrived in Dhaka as a stranger, and clinicians re-ordered tests that had been run days earlier — an estimated ৳31 crore a year in duplicated diagnostics.

Their telemedicine service, launched hurriedly, ran on a consumer video tool with no record of what was prescribed, which made follow-up care guesswork and audit impossible.

How we approached it

01

Clinical workflow mapping

Twelve days observing outpatient, inpatient and emergency flows, mapping every handoff where information was lost between departments.

02

A single patient identity

A master patient index resolving duplicate records across four legacy systems, with a clinician-reviewed merge queue for ambiguous matches.

03

Telemedicine inside the record

Consultations, prescriptions and follow-ups written into the same record as in-person visits, with e-prescriptions that pharmacies can verify.

04

Rollout by department

Registration and outpatient first, then diagnostics, pharmacy, inpatient and finance, each with a week of on-site support per facility.

What we built

  • Master patient index resolving 1.1 million records across four legacy systems
  • Teleconsultation with recording, e-prescription and follow-up scheduling
  • Diagnostic ordering with duplicate-test warnings at the point of order
  • Bangla clinical interface with structured coding underneath
  • Role-based access with full audit trail for every record view
  • Bed, theatre and equipment utilisation dashboards for administrators
