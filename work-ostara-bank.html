Retail banking rebuilt around the 92% who never enter a branch

A full redesign and rebuild of Ostara Bank’s retail app and onboarding, taking account opening from eleven days to nine minutes.

The challenge

Ostara had 4.2 million account holders and a mobile app rated 2.9 stars. Eighty-one percent of support calls concerned two things: failed logins and a fund transfer screen that gave no indication whether money had moved.

Account opening still required a branch visit and eleven working days. Competitors were onboarding customers in a single session from a phone, and Ostara was losing roughly 14,000 prospective accounts a month to that gap.

How we approached it

01

Field research in branches

Nine days of observation across four branches in Dhaka and Sylhet, shadowing 26 customers and 11 relationship officers to find where the paper process actually slowed down.

02

Rebuilt onboarding on e-KYC

A nine-minute flow with NID verification, liveness check and automated risk scoring, designed so a failed step explains itself instead of dead-ending.

03

New transfer architecture

An idempotent transaction service with clear pending, settled and failed states surfaced directly in the interface, replacing a silent queue.

04

Staged migration

The new app shipped to 5% of users, then 20%, then all, with the legacy build kept available for six weeks so no customer was forced to move mid-transaction.

What we built

  • Nine-minute e-KYC onboarding with NID and liveness verification
  • Offline-tolerant transaction queue with explicit state on every screen
  • Biometric and device-bound authentication replacing SMS OTP as default
  • Bangla-first interface with full English parity
  • Accessibility conformance to WCAG 2.2 AA across all core journeys
  • Real-time fraud scoring integrated at the point of transfer
