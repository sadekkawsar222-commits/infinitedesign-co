Talk about SaaS Application Development

Building a SaaS product is a different discipline from building software for one company. Tenancy, metering, self-serve onboarding, plan changes, dunning, churn instrumentation — these decide whether the product survives its second year.

We build with a founder, not for one. That means an architecture that can be handed to your own team, and a roadmap shaped by activation and retention data rather than a feature wish list.

What the work includes

  • Product discovery, scoping and MVP definition
  • Multi-tenant architecture and data isolation
  • Subscription billing, metering and invoicing
  • Self-serve onboarding and activation flows
  • Admin consoles, impersonation and support tooling
  • Usage analytics, cohort and churn instrumentation
  • SOC-friendly audit logging and data export
  • Scaling plan with cost per tenant modelled

What you receive

  • Launch-ready product with billing live
  • Tenant provisioning and support console
  • Activation and retention analytics dashboards
  • Technical roadmap and hiring plan for your team
