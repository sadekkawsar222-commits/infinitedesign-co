Route intelligence for 1,800 riders in the world’s most congested city

A dispatch and routing platform using live traffic and delivery-history data to sequence last-mile deliveries across Dhaka.

Location

Dhaka, Bangladesh

The challenge

Rundo’s dispatchers assigned parcels by neighbourhood and instinct. A rider in Mirpur could be routed across the city at four in the afternoon, and the same address could be attempted three times because failed deliveries carried no history.

Their cost per delivery had risen for six consecutive quarters, and the first-attempt success rate sat at 71% — every failure costing a second trip through the same traffic.

How we approached it

01

Learn from their own history

Eighteen months of delivery records became the training set: which addresses fail at which hours, which buildings need a phone call first, where the map is simply wrong.

02

Routing that understands Dhaka

A solver combining live traffic, historical travel times by hour and rider-reported road conditions, tuned for a city where a 4 km trip can take ninety minutes.

03

A rider app built for one hand

Large targets, offline capability, voice-guided sequencing and a two-tap failure reason that feeds straight back into the model.

04

Continuous evaluation

Weekly comparison of predicted against actual travel time per zone, with the model retrained monthly on fresh data.

What we built

  • Route optimisation using live traffic and 18 months of delivery history
  • Address risk scoring predicting first-attempt failure before dispatch
  • Rider app with offline sequencing, voice guidance and one-hand operation
  • Automated customer notification windows narrowed to 90 minutes
  • Live control tower with exception alerts for dispatchers
  • Cost-per-delivery analytics broken down by zone and rider
