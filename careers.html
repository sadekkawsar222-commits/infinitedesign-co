Why here

What is actually different about working at this studio.

Two projects, maximum

Nobody is spread across five engagements. You get enough context to do the work properly and enough time to care about it.

Learning budget that gets used

৳60,000 per person per year plus ten working days, and a founder who asks in your review whether you took them.

Genuinely flexible hours

Core overlap 11:00–16:00, the rest is yours. Hybrid by default, fully remote for the right person.

Health cover for your family

Medical cover including spouse and children, plus paid parental leave for all parents — from day one, not after probation.

Nobody stops shipping

All four founders still write code or push pixels every week. There is no management layer to be promoted into, and that is deliberate.

Work that leaves Bangladesh

About a third of our projects serve clients in the UAE, the UK and Australia — with your name on the commit history and your face in the client call.

Hiring process

Four steps, about two weeks.

We pay for the take-home exercise and we give feedback whether or not you get an offer.

01

Application review

2–4 days

Your CV and, more usefully, anything you have made. We read every application ourselves — no automated screening.

02

Conversation

Week 1

45 minutes with the person who would lead your work. Mostly about how you think and what you have shipped.

03

Paid exercise

Week 1–2

A small, realistic problem — four hours, paid at your day rate. Never spec work for a real client.

04

Team session

Week 2

Two hours with the team you would join, walking through your exercise and ours. Offer or feedback within 48 hours.
