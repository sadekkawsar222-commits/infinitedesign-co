Case studies Energy & Utilities

Fourteen thousand rooftop systems, watched from one screen

An IoT telemetry and analytics platform monitoring rooftop solar installations, predicting faults before customers notice a shortfall.

The challenge

Cirrus installed rooftop solar across industrial and residential sites but learned about underperformance only when a customer complained — typically two to three months after output had quietly dropped.

Engineers were dispatched on guesswork. Around 40% of site visits found nothing wrong, while genuinely failing inverters ran degraded for weeks.

How we approached it

01

Telemetry that survives the network

A store-and-forward gateway holding fourteen days of readings locally, so rural sites with intermittent connectivity lose nothing.

02

A baseline per installation

Expected output modelled per site from orientation, tilt, shading and local irradiance, so deviation means something specific rather than generic.

03

Fault classification

Models distinguishing soiling, shading, string failure and inverter degradation, each mapped to a different engineer action.

04

Dispatch by confidence

Work orders raised automatically with a predicted fault and confidence score, so engineers arrive knowing what to bring.

What we built

  • Store-and-forward gateways tolerating 14 days offline
  • Per-installation output baselines from orientation, tilt and shading
  • Fault classification separating soiling, shading and inverter failure
  • Automated work orders with predicted fault and confidence score
  • Customer portal showing generation, savings and carbon offset
  • Fleet dashboards with performance ranking by district and installer
